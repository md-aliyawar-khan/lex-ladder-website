Our Legal Services

Lex Ladder supports clients across the critical pillars of modern law—from preventive counsel and transactional work to recovery, dispute resolution, and courtroom advocacy. Each engagement is tailored to your facts, sector, and objectives, with a consistent focus on clarity, strategy, and measurable progress.

Legal Consulting & Strategic Advisory

We provide structured legal consulting for individuals and organisations facing decisions that carry legal, financial, or reputational weight. That includes early risk assessment, regulatory and compliance guidance, pre-litigation strategy, and second opinions on ongoing matters. Our aim is to give you a clear picture of rights, obligations, and practical options so you can act with confidence—not guesswork.

Contracts, Agreements & Due Diligence

We draft, review, and negotiate contracts and commercial agreements with an emphasis on precision and enforceability. Whether you are entering a partnership, closing a transaction, hiring key talent, or formalising recurring commercial relationships, we focus on terms that protect you when relationships are strong and defend you if they fracture. Our due diligence support helps uncover legal, contractual, and operational red flags before you commit capital or liability.

Money Recovery & Enforcement

Unpaid dues, breached payment terms, and stalled recoveries can erode trust and cash flow. We pursue result-driven money recovery strategies appropriate to your case—from demand and structured negotiation to formal recovery mechanisms available under law. We align our approach with your commercial goals, whether that means preserving a business relationship or pursuing full enforcement.

Criminal Law Defence

Criminal allegations demand immediate, disciplined defence. We represent clients with a focus on protecting liberty, dignity, and procedural fairness. Our work includes bail applications, trial preparation, examination of evidence, and robust representation before courts and investigating authorities. You receive candid advice on strengths, vulnerabilities, and the strategic choices that can shape the outcome of your case.

Civil Litigation & Disputes

We handle a broad range of civil matters—including property, contracts, financial disputes, and related civil wrongs—with an emphasis on thorough preparation and persuasive advocacy. Where early settlement serves your interests, we pursue it; where litigation is necessary, we build a record and arguments designed to withstand scrutiny and advance your position.

Family Law

Family disputes require both legal rigour and human judgment. We advise and represent clients in matters such as matrimonial disputes, maintenance, custody and access, and related issues—always with attention to sensitivity, privacy, and the long-term welfare of those involved. Our goal is to help you navigate emotionally charged situations with clear legal guidance and a path toward stability.

Dispute Resolution & Mediation

Not every conflict belongs in prolonged litigation. We assist with negotiation, mediation, and structured dispute resolution where appropriate, helping parties find durable solutions while controlling cost and uncertainty. When alternative resolution is not viable, we are prepared to advocate forcefully in forums where your rights are decided.

To discuss which services apply to your situation, contact us to arrange a consultation.
